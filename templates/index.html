🚀 Getting Started

1

Launch the App

Run the application and open it in your browser. The camera will start automatically.

2

Show Your Hand

Position your hand in front of the webcam. The system will detect it and start tracking your gestures.

3

Use Built-in Gestures

Point, pinch, scroll, and more — built-in gestures work immediately without any training.

4

Create Custom Gestures

Go to the Gestures tab to create new gestures, record samples, then train the AI model on the Training tab.
